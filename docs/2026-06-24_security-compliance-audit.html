What Got Fixed

The June 22 audit flagged two required fixes. Both shipped across four releases as part of the move to Vercel.

Resolved in this cycle

  • Edge security headers — migrated to Vercel with a real vercel.json: HSTS (with preload), X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, Referrer-Policy and a locked-down Permissions-Policy.
  • Content-Security-Policy — a full policy, scoped to the Instagram widget and Google Fonts, promoted from Report-Only to enforcing.
  • Privacy Policy — published at /privacy, linked in the footer, written to be CalOPPA / CCPA-aware (California rights, no sale of data, contact channel).
  • Clean separation — internal engineering reports removed from the deployed site; they now live as records only.

Compliance — What Actually Applies

The Rules Governing This Site

A single-location restaurant marketing site with a catering enquiry form. The real framework is the ADA (via the website "nexus" to the physical restaurant), the WCAG accessibility guidelines courts rely on, and California's privacy and civil-rights statutes.

RequirementApplies?Where We Stand
ADA Title III — websiteReachableVia the 9th-Circuit "nexus" rule (Robles v. Domino's). WCAG is the de-facto benchmark.

Already Solid

What's Working In Your Favor

  • ✓
    No backend, no database — a static Vite/React build. The catering form opens the visitor's own email app (mailto) — nothing is stored on a La Vida server, so there is no database to breach and no intake to inject into.
  • ✓
    Privacy-friendly by default — no Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising cookies. There is very little personal data to govern in the first place.
  • ✓
    Payments fully offloaded — ordering and card payment run entirely on Toast (pickup) and Grubhub / DoorDash (delivery); the site never touches payment data, keeping PCI scope at zero.
  • ✓
    Strong accessibility baseline — skip-to-content link, visible focus rings, AAA-level contrast on the olive brand color, semantic landmarks, labelled form fields, full prefers-reduced-motion support, and a dedicated Accessibility page in the footer.
  • ✓
    Client-side form validation — the catering form is validated with React Hook Form + Zod before it ever opens an email.
