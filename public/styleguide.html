Motion

Motion is gentle and brand-led. The signature is Coin Shine — a light sweep that travels across the logo and primary button on a 15-second loop, echoing the circular "coin" logo. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Coin Shine (signature)

.animate-coin-shine / .animate-button-shine

A light streak sweeps the logo, travels the gap, then sweeps the CTA — forward then reverse — over a 15s cycle. Demo sped up to ~5s.
